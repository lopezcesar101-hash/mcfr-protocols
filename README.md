# MCFR Protocols — Fast Lookup

A fast lookup webapp for the **Monroe County Fire Rescue EMS Protocols, 2026 Version**.

- Search all 82 protocols and 43 formulary medications from the official 2026 book
- Browse by category (Standing Orders, BLS, ALS Medical, Cardiac, Cardiac Arrest,
  Toxicology, Chemical Control, Environmental, Trauma, Obstetrical, Medication Formulary)
- Every entry shows its book page; chart pages (cardiac arrest algorithms, START/JumpSTART
  triage, trauma alert criteria) are shown as images of the official book pages
- All content is taken directly from the official protocol book — nothing added or paraphrased

Lookup aid only. Always confirm against the official protocol book and current medical
direction before acting.
